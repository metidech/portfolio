01 / Healthcare

First Care Dental Clinic

Dental · Al Nahda, Sharjah

A website for a busy dental clinic, designed to turn a Google search into a phone call by leading with the trust signals patients actually look for.

Visit Live Website ↗
5.0 Google rating displayed
Click-to-call & WhatsApp
Overview +

First Care Dental Clinic is a family dental practice in Al Nahda, Sharjah. I designed and built their website to give new patients a clear, credible first impression online — one that reflects the same care they'd receive walking through the clinic door.

Challenge +

Most patients searching for a dentist compare a handful of clinics on their phone before ever calling one. The site needed to win that comparison in seconds — establishing trust immediately and removing any friction between "found it" and "called it."

Research +

I looked at how other dental clinics in Sharjah and Dubai present themselves online, and noticed how often genuine trust signals — a real rating, real reviews, a visible phone number — were buried below decorative content instead of leading the page.

Goals +
  • Build credibility with a new visitor in the first few seconds
  • Make calling or messaging the clinic effortless from any page
  • Keep the experience fast and simple to browse on a phone
Design Decisions +

A calm, clinical colour palette with soft, close-up imagery instead of stock smiles. The clinic's 5.0 Google rating and review count sit directly in the hero, not in a footer — the first thing a visitor's eye lands on after the headline.

Wireframes +

Before any visual design, I mapped the homepage as a simple content hierarchy — trust signal, headline, reassurance, call to action — the same structure the finished page still follows.

Design Process +

01Discovery conversation with the clinic about patients and priorities.
02Wireframed the page structure around trust and contact.
03Designed the visual layer — colour, type, imagery.
04Built and tested the live site across devices.

Development +

Built with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript, and deployed on Netlify. No unnecessary frameworks — kept dependency-free so the site stays fast and easy to maintain.

Technologies +
HTMLTailwind CSSJavaScriptNetlify
Key Features +
Google reviews widgetClick-to-callWhatsApp buttonServices overview
Responsive Design +

Designed mobile-first, since most patients arrive from a phone search. Layout, type size and tap targets were checked and adjusted across common phone, tablet and desktop widths before launch.

Accessibility +

Semantic heading order, sufficient colour contrast on text and buttons, descriptive alt text on images, and fully keyboard-reachable call-to-action buttons.

SEO +

Descriptive page titles and meta descriptions, a clear heading hierarchy, and the clinic's name, address and phone number presented consistently — the basics that help a local business get found.

Performance +

No heavy frameworks, compressed imagery, and minimal JavaScript — the goal was a page that opens instantly on an average mobile connection, not just a impressive demo on fast wifi.

Lessons Learned +

Working on a healthcare site reinforced how much small trust signals — a real rating, a visible phone number — matter more to a first-time visitor than any amount of decoration.

Future Improvements +

Next on the roadmap for this project: an online appointment request form, and a short local-SEO blog to help the clinic rank for more Sharjah-area searches.
